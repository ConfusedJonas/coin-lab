Why should this approach ⅓?

HH, HT, TH, and TT are equally likely. “At least one head” excludes TT, leaving three outcomes. Only HH has two heads: 1 out of 3. For tails, exclude HH instead. Again, 1 out of 3.

Where does ½ come from?

If you know the penny specifically is heads, only HH and HT remain. The dime is heads in 1 out of 2. That is different information from “at least one coin is heads.” Small samples fluctuate; more flips tend toward the theoretical values.
